AURA Multi-Vehicle Warehouse Simulation Guide

🏢 Campus Layout

The logistics campus comprises Warehouse 1 (West, Docks A & B), Warehouse 2 (Central, Docks C & D), and Warehouse 3 (East, Docks E & F), plus a dedicated Charging Yard (Bays CH-01, CH-02, CH-03).

🚚 Autonomous Vehicle Fleet

3 independently controlled AMRs (AMR-01, AMR-02, AMR-03) operate concurrently with independent A* path planning, bicycle kinematics, and mutual collision deconfliction.

🔄 Mission Lifecycle & Worker Approval Gates

  1. IDLE_AT_CHARGER → TRAVELLING_TO_PICKUP
  2. LOADING (progresses over 5 simulation seconds)
  3. WAITING_FOR_LOAD_APPROVAL (vehicle remains stopped until worker approves departure)
  4. TRAVELLING_TO_DELIVERY → UNLOADING (5 simulation seconds)
  5. WAITING_FOR_UNLOAD_APPROVAL (worker approves delivery)
  6. RETURNING_TO_CHARGER → CHARGING (+0.3% SoC/sec)

👁️ Viewport View Modes

  • 3D: Perspective overview or third-person follow camera
  • TOPO: True orthographic top-down campus map
  • LAYER: Interactive layer toggles (buildings, aisles, footprints, sensor cones, trajectories)
  • HEATMAP: Live traffic density heatmap computed from simulation occupancy history
  • TRAJECTORY: Planned paths (cyan), executed trail (purple), reroute paths (coral), and ETAs
